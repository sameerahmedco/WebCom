# WebCom Community Conduct

WebCom's public project spaces are for constructive technical collaboration. Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, critique ideas and code rather than people, avoid harassment or discriminatory conduct, and protect private/security-sensitive information.

Do not publish another person's personal information, credentials, private browsing data, or uncoordinated vulnerability details. Security reports belong in the private process described by `SECURITY.md`.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove disruptive content and may restrict participation when needed to keep project spaces safe and useful. Good-faith disagreement, bug reports, technical criticism, and discussion of tradeoffs are welcome when they remain professional and relevant.

For platform-level abuse or safety concerns, use GitHub's own reporting and moderation controls in addition to any project-maintainer contact shown in the repository.
